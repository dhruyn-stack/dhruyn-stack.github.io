Card Library

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

The short version

  • Your notes stay on your iPad. We never see them.
  • Only the study café uses the internet. It keeps the little it needs to work: your café name, your avatar, and who your study buddies are.
  • No ads. No tracking. We never sell or share your data for advertising.

Who we are: Card Library is made by an independent developer (the seller named on the App Store page). Contact: dhruyn@gmail.com.

1. What stays on your iPad

Notebooks, handwriting, typed text, topic cards, PDFs, pictures, camera scans, voice notes, flashcard progress and settings are stored only on your iPad. We never receive them. A backup goes only where you choose to save it (for example the Files app or iCloud Drive), under your own Apple account.

The camera is used only when you take a photo or scan a page into a notebook. The microphone is used only when you record a voice note, or when you switch voice on in a private café.

Card Sync with your own computer works over your Wi-Fi, straight between your devices. It never passes through our server.

Cards from the web: a card you add can name files on the internet (fonts, pictures, chart tools). The app downloads those files once, so the website that hosts them sees a normal download request. A video in a card plays from YouTube or Vimeo only when you tap it, and their own privacy policy applies while it plays.

2. What the study café keeps on our server

When you first open the café, the app makes an anonymous café account: no email, no phone number, no password. The café server (Supabase, hosted in Mumbai, India) keeps:

DataWhy
A random account idTo know which seat, buddies and messages are yours
Your café name and avatar lookShown to other students on your profile
Your streak, and whether you are focusing or on a breakShown on your profile and seat
Study buddies, buddy requests, blocksSo buddies and blocking work
Messages to buddies and in private cafésSo they can be delivered
Cards you choose to share, and who you shared them withSo the other student can open them (in safe view)
Private cafés you made or joinedSo the room code and members work
Reports you send, with the last few messages of that chatSo we can deal with abuse

The café never keeps what you study, your notes, or your location.

Voice and "Show my page" are live only. Voice in a private café goes through LiveKit, a voice relay service, while you talk. "Show my page" goes through the café server while you show it. Neither is recorded or saved.

3. Who else handles the data

  • Supabase stores the café data above and passes live café updates.
  • LiveKit passes voice in private cafés, live only.

Both handle the data only to run the café for us, and protect it at least as well as this policy says. We use no analytics, advertising or tracking services.

4. How long we keep it

  • Café data stays until you delete your café account. Then it is removed straight away.
  • Reports are kept for up to 12 months after we have dealt with them, so we can act on repeat abuse. The account ids in a report are removed when that account is deleted.

5. Delete your data

  • In the app: Library › More › About & privacy › Delete my café account. Or inside the café: tap yourself › Change name › Delete my café account.
  • By email: write to dhruyn@gmail.com and we delete it within 30 days.
  • Your notes on the iPad are yours: delete them in the app, or delete the app.

6. Children

The café is for students aged 13 and older. Card Library is not made for children under 13, and we do not knowingly keep data from them. If you think a child under 13 uses the café, email us and we will delete the account.

7. Safety in the café

Students agree to the café rules before they join. Every profile and chat has Report and Block. We read reports within 24 hours and remove people who break the rules.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, we update this page and the date at the top. Big changes are also shown in the app.
