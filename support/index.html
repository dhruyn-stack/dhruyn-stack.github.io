iPad app

Lekhin Notes

Your handwriting, your topic cards and a study café, in one calm place to study.

Need help?

Email support@lekhinnotes.com. We usually answer within two days, and reports about the café within 24 hours.

What it does

  • The study café: tap the cup on any page, take a seat and study next to other students with a focus timer. After 60 minutes at the same table you can become study buddies. Make a private café for friends with a room code, with voice if you want it.
  • Write like paper on topic cards, notebooks and PDFs with Apple Pencil: pen, highlighter, tape to hide answers, lasso, shapes, text, voice notes.
  • Circle to Copy and Clips: circle any part of a page and copy it, or keep it in Clips and search the words inside.
  • Topic cards and canvas: every topic card keeps its own notebook; lay out cards, notebooks and sticky notes on one canvas.

Common questions

Where are my notes stored?

Only on your iPad. Make a backup from Library › More › Back up everything and keep it in Files or iCloud Drive.

How do I move my notes to a new iPad?

On the old iPad: Library › More › Back up everything. On the new iPad: Library › More › Restore from a backup.

How do I delete my café account?

Library › More › About & privacy › Delete my café account. Your notes on the iPad are not touched.

Someone in the café is rude. What do I do?

Tap them, then Block to hide them from you at once, and Report so we can act. We read reports within 24 hours.

Can other students see what I study?

No. They see your café name, your avatar, your streak and whether you are focusing or on a break. Your page is shown only when you choose Show my page, and only to the members of the private café you pick.
